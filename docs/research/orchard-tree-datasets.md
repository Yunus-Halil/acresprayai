# Orchard and row-crop datasets with per-plant labels — catalogue

Compiled 2026-10-19 for Swardus crown detection on nadir RGB orthomosaics at 1–3 cm/px.
Scope: public UAV/aerial RGB imagery with per-tree (point, box, mask) or per-plant labels.
Every licence quoted below was read on the dataset's own repository page on 2026-10-19; where a page could not be opened the licence is marked **unverified**. Nothing was downloaded. "n/s" = not stated on the page.

Licence shorthand: **CC BY 4.0** permits commercial use and derivative works (a trained model is an adaptation) with attribution. **CC BY-SA 4.0** permits commercial use but adaptations must carry the same licence; treat as contaminating for a proprietary model. **CC0** needs nothing. **NC** forbids commercial use.

Suitability codes: **E** = usable to evaluate a crown detector at 1–3 cm GSD; **T** = usable to train one; **E-** / **T-** = partially (wrong GSD, wrong canopy structure, too small); **no** = not usable.

## Findings

1. **Orchard-specific, open, 1–3 cm, per-tree labels are rare.** The verified matches are OliveTreeCrownsDb (1.78 cm, boxes, CC BY 4.0, one Moroccan field) and ReforesTree (2 cm, boxes, CC BY 4.0, machine-assisted labels, tropical agroforestry). The same Moroccan group's 2026 olive / apricot / vineyard release (A2) is CC BY 4.0 and may add vineyards, but its page shows almost nothing and must be inspected before being relied on. Apple, citrus, almond and nursery UAV sets with crown labels were **not found** in open repositories; citrus exists only as unlabelled USDA frames with per-tree ground data (A6), which would be a strong evaluation set if we draw about 1,300 boxes ourselves.
2. **Fields are few.** OliveTreeCrownsDb is one field; ReforesTree is 6 plots; PalmDSNet splits by site. Any metric from A1 is a single-field metric and must be reported as such.
3. **Large permissive tree-crown corpora exist at 4.5–10 cm/px** (OAM-TCD masks, SelvaBox boxes, NEON boxes), forest and urban. They suit pretraining a crown head, then fine-tuning on A1 / A3 and our own orchards; the resolution gap to 1–3 cm is 3–5×.
4. **Row crops.** For corn, the only verified open per-plant UAV set is INRAE UAVmulticrop (C1, CC BY 4.0, ~5.5 k maize boxes at 2.5 mm/px). For **soybean, wheat (plants, not heads) and cotton (plants, not weeds), no open UAV per-plant dataset was found**; papers exist but publish no data. Treat these as self-labelled targets.
5. **Licence hazards for a commercial model:** CropAndWeed and CWFID (non-commercial), PhenoBench (SA plus an NC-labelled README), BAMFORESTS (no dataset licence shown), Kaggle palms and Roboflow Universe projects (unread or variable). MillionTrees must be filtered per source.

## Recommended next step

Evaluate first on **OliveTreeCrownsDb** (native 1.78 cm; single field, report it as such). Train or fine-tune with **ReforesTree + OAM-TCD (CC BY subset) + SelvaBox**, tiled or resampled toward our GSD. Label the **USDA Fort Pierce citrus** frames ourselves (read the licence once the page is reachable) to gain a second, row-structured orchard with per-tree ground truth. For corn use UAVmulticrop downsampled 5–10×; for soybean, wheat and cotton, plan our own flights.

Unverified items (page not opened or licence not shown): A2 contents, A6 licence, A8, A9, B2 overall licence, B5 dataset licence, C4, C6, and the two arXiv / DataPort entries in table D.
