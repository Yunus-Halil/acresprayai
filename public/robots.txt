# Public marketing pages are open to everything. The signed-in surfaces are not:
# the SPA rewrite means /app/* and /admin/* return 200 with the app shell rather
# than a 404, so without these rules a crawler that found the URL could index an
# empty shell under the homepage's title. The pages themselves also emit
# <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> - see src/components/Seo.tsx.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /app/
Disallow: /admin/
Disallow: /auth

Sitemap: https://swathwise.com/sitemap.xml
